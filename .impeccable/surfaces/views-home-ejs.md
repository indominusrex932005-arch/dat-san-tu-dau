# Surface

## Scope

Website-wide redesign for customer, owner and admin flows. Preserve routes, content, data and role behavior.

## Visitor mode

Primarily Operate: visitors need to find and reserve courts quickly; home and facilities pages persuade action. The system should feel professional, restrained and trustworthy.

## Audience and job

- Customers find a facility and court, select 30-minute slots and complete the hold/payment flow.
- Guests can place an order using name and phone.
- Owners scan bookings and manage the court operation.
- Admins verify and assign facilities.

## Chosen direction

THESIS: A sports operations board for one task at a time: identify a venue, read availability, and act without decorative friction.

OWN-WORLD: Deep forest ink, warm white canvas, thin structural rules, gold for selected or important times, brick red for warnings. Dense information is organized into a disciplined timetable with no nested cards.

STORY: The visitor first understands where and when to play, then sees real facilities and courts, then reaches a compact booking summary.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A working booking entry leads the home page: navigation, headline, facility/date search and a real court availability preview. Primary action “Tìm sân trống” remains visible. Below: actual facilities.

FORM: Use a flat, editorial operations-board form; strong hierarchy, full borders and tabular data. Seed reference seed:501ae3719bb0b6cd.
